About Me

I’m D. K. Charan Raj, a Unity Certified Associate Game Developer and a 3D designer specializing in immersive virtual experiences. I focus on building interactive systems that blend virtual reality, narrative design, and real-time environments to create meaningful user experiences.

My work primarily revolves around VR development, 3D modeling, and game design, where I design and develop applications that go beyond visuals—focusing on usability, interaction, and storytelling. I’ve worked on projects ranging from virtual campus simulations and AI-driven characters to industrial training environments, each aimed at solving real-world problems through immersive technology.
