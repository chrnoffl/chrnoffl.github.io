Charaka Herbarium

A Virtual Reality–Based Digital Herbal Knowledge System

Project Overview

← Back to Portfolio

Charaka Herbarium is a Virtual Reality–based digital herbal garden designed to preserve, visualize, and educate users about medicinal plants described in traditional Indian medicine. The project recreates a curated herbal environment in VR, allowing users to explore plants interactively.

Key Features

  • Virtual Herbal Garden Environment: Fully immersive VR space.
  • Interactive Plant Information: Detailed medicinal properties.
  • Educational Experience: Designed for students and researchers.
